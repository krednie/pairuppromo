# PairUp Founding Charm Assets

The files in `public/assets/badges` are 360 x 360 WebP derivatives prepared for
the early-access charm picker. They are intentionally small and served locally;
the live interface does not call third-party image hosts.

## Production Gate

Before a commercial launch, replace the five prototype-only product images
with commissioned illustrations, user-provided licensed files, or written
permissions. Keep the object references, but do not ship unlicensed official
product photography as permanent production artwork.
